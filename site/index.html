What Nirvana helps with

Eight quiet companions for one simple goal: showing up a little bit, every day.

💧

Hydration

Quick-log buttons, a daily progress ring, and reminders that ease off automatically as bedtime approaches.

🦵

Knee & joint care

Log pain, swelling, and stiffness in seconds. Nirvana gently suggests seeing a professional when warning signs show up — never to push through pain.

🧘

Dhyana (meditation)

A soft, distraction-free timer with a start and end bell. Breath awareness, body scan, or simple counting.

🏋️

Exercise

Guided knee-friendly strength sessions with sets, reps, timers, and a pain check-in after every session.

🍽️

Food

Simple meal logging against sensible portion templates with a rough protein estimate — no calorie obsession.

⚖️

Weight

Log weigh-ins and see your 7-day average and trend, because a single day's number is noise.

🖥️

Desk breaks

Quiet, configurable nudges to look away, stand up, and move your knees and neck during long screen time.

📈

Progress

See your trends across weight, pain, and consistency over time.

Philosophy

Consistency, not perfection

Nirvana is built around one simple idea: a good day isn't a perfect diet and a punishing workout — it's staying hydrated, moving a little, and showing up again tomorrow.

  • ✓ No shame, ever. Missed a day? That's fine — tomorrow is still there.
  • ✓ Your data stays on your device. Nirvana has no backend and makes no network requests.
  • ✓ Every schedule is yours to configure — reminder times, intervals, and goals aren't locked in.
  • ✓ Nirvana never diagnoses or treats any condition — a lifestyle companion, not a medical device.
🌿
Nirvana has no backend and makes no network requests.

Everything you log — hydration, pain check-ins, meditation sessions, meals, weight, and reminders — stays in a local database on your device only.
